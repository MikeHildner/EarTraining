# Privacy Policy — Ear Training

**Effective date:** June 27, 2026

Ear Training ("the app") is a free ear-training app published by Mike Hildner.
This policy explains how the app handles your information. The short version:
**the app collects nothing and sends nothing.**

## Information we collect

**None.** Ear Training does not collect, store, or transmit any personal
information. Specifically, it has:

- No user accounts or sign-in.
- No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting.
- No advertising or ad networks.
- No third-party trackers or SDKs that collect data.

## Network use

The app works **fully offline**. It makes no network connections and sends no
data off your device. All audio, music notation, and scoring run locally on
your device.

## Data stored on your device

Any settings or scores stay **on your device only** and are never uploaded.
Deleting the app removes them.

## Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data from anyone, it
collects no data from children.

## Permissions

The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone,
photos, or any other personal data.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a
new effective date.
